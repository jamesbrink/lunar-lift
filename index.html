The S-1 solid booster burns at 100% once lit and sheds itself at burnout; the S-2 and CSM engines are throttled with ⇧ / CTRL. Chase the green trajectory line: pitch toward the Moon while climbing (gravity turn), build an apoapsis past the Moon's dashed sphere of influence, then flip V + P and burn retrograde at encounter to land softer than 32 m/s.

Controls are inertial: the keys apply torque, so the fully fueled stack turns slowly and control authority climbs as propellant burns off.
