The S-1 solid booster burns at full thrust once lit and drops itself at burnout; the S-2 and CSM engines throttle. SAS flies the gravity turn for you and then holds the velocity vector — prograde to speed up, retrograde to brake. In thick air keep the nose near the airflow: the side load readout turns red before the stack breaks.

The keys apply torque, so a full stack turns slowly and gets livelier as it empties. With RCS assist off nothing damps a rotation in vacuum but you.
